Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how KYNX (“KYNX”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information when you use Zuri, our AI assistant (“Zuri” or “the App”).

1. Information we collect

Account information. When you create or use a Zuri account, we may process information needed to authenticate and maintain your account, such as your email address and authentication identifiers.

Content you provide. We process information you submit to Zuri, including prompts, messages, files, images, audio or voice input, and other content you choose to send to the App.

Generated content. Zuri may create text, images, audio, video, research results, or other content in response to your requests. Generated content may be processed and stored so it can be delivered to you and, where applicable, associated with your conversation.

Technical information. The App and the services it uses may process technical information needed to provide, secure, and maintain the service, such as device and network information, app activity, and diagnostic information where available.

2. How we use information

  • To create and authenticate your Zuri account.
  • To provide Zuri's AI assistant and respond to your requests.
  • To store and display your conversations and generated content where the App provides those features.
  • To process uploaded or generated media and deliver it to you.
  • To maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the service.
  • To prevent abuse, fraud, or unauthorized access.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Service providers and sharing

To operate Zuri, information may be transmitted to third-party service providers that process information on our behalf. Depending on the feature you use, these may include authentication, database/storage, AI, media-generation, media-storage, hosting, and infrastructure providers.

We only use information shared with service providers as necessary to provide, secure, or operate the relevant service. We do not sell your personal information.

4. AI processing

When you send a prompt, conversation message, file, image, audio, or other input to an AI-powered feature, the relevant content may be transmitted to AI or infrastructure providers needed to generate the requested response. Do not submit highly sensitive information unless you are comfortable with it being processed to provide the requested feature.

5. Voice and audio

If you use Zuri voice features, audio may be processed to provide the requested voice or speech functionality. Zuri only accesses microphone input when the relevant feature is used and the required device permission has been granted.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Information transmitted between the App and supported services is protected using secure network connections where available.

7. Data retention

We retain account information and user content for as long as necessary to provide Zuri and maintain the user's account, unless a shorter period is required or a user requests deletion.

When an account deletion request is processed, we delete the account and associated user data that we control, subject to information that must be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or other legitimate legal purposes. Any information retained for such purposes is kept only for as long as necessary and then deleted or anonymised.

9. Children's privacy

Zuri is not directed at children under the minimum age required to use the service under applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, data practices, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
